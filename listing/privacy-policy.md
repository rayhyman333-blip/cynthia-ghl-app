Cynthia, by Planet Leads ("Cynthia", "we"), is an app for the
HighLevel Marketplace that lets an agency give its client businesses a
verified-answer AI inside their HighLevel sub-account.

## What Cynthia processes

- **Questions** typed into Cynthia's page inside HighLevel, and the
  verified answers returned for them.
- **Install information** HighLevel sends us when the app is
  installed or uninstalled: the location (sub-account) ID, agency
  (company) ID, and user ID of the installer.
- **User context** HighLevel provides to the app's page (user ID,
  agency ID, location ID, role, name, email) so the page knows which
  location it is serving. This is decrypted on our servers and used
  only to route the request.
- **OAuth tokens** issued by HighLevel for the installed location or
  agency, stored so the app can maintain the installation.

## What Cynthia does not do

- Cynthia does not read your contacts, conversations, calendars, or
  other HighLevel data. The app requests no HighLevel data scopes.
- Cynthia does not sell personal data, and does not use questions or
  answers to train models. Cynthia's answers come only from facts the
  business itself approved; she does not generate answers from
  personal data.
- Cynthia does not store payment card information. Marketplace
  billing is handled by HighLevel.

## Retention and deletion

When the app is uninstalled from a location, its OAuth tokens are
deleted and the location is marked uninstalled. Business fact stores
provisioned for a client are that client's own Cynthia deployment and
are removed when the client's service with Planet Leads ends.
